Market size

Where in the world is
nuclear fusion money spent?

NORTH AMERICA - 48%

North America leads, with most private fusion startups, the largest rounds raised so far, and the first deals to sell fusion power to tech companies.

EUROPE - 24%

Europe relies on public programs and a few startups, with ITER in France as its anchor and national labs in the UK and Germany behind most of the work.

ASIA-PACIFIC - 21%

Asia-Pacific grows with China and Japan, where the state funds large test reactors and moves faster than most private projects in the West.

LATIN AMERICA - 2%

Latin America has almost no fusion spending yet, outside of a few university research labs in Brazil and Argentina.

MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA - 5%

The Middle East funds a few partnerships with Western startups, mostly to prepare the energy mix that comes after oil and gas.

Where in the world is
biotechnology money spent?

NORTH AMERICA - 44%

North America leads by far, with Boston and San Francisco as the two main hubs for biotech startups, investors, and big pharma buyers.

EUROPE - 25%

Europe is strong in research, with hubs in the UK, Switzerland, and Germany, but its startups raise less money and often sell out early.

ASIA-PACIFIC - 23%

LATIN AMERICA - 4%

Latin America is small and mostly buys drugs and tests made elsewhere, with a few local makers in Brazil and Argentina.

MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA - 4%

The Middle East invests in local production and research centers, while Africa remains a small market that mostly buys from abroad.
