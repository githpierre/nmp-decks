# Advanced nuclear: signal research for extra slides

**Cutoff:** November 18, 2026  
**Qualified candidates:** 14  
**Market opportunity candidates:** 6  
**Format cap:** two candidates maximum per format  
**Scope:** novel nuclear **fission** reactors, SMRs, microreactors, dedicated reactor-specific fuel, recycling and enrichment. Fusion, conventional-plant operation, generic uranium mining and generic data-center power services are excluded.

## Decision rules and important caveats

- **A development agreement, a right, a project proposal and a conditional pipeline are not a firm reactor order.** We never add overlapping announcements to manufacture a capacity total.
- **Permitted is not built; criticality is not electricity delivery.** The DOE 2026 results cited here were zero-power test milestones.
- **Financial prices are point-in-time.** Equity market capitalizations, share prices and price-to-sales ratios use the November 17, 2026 U.S. close; filings cover the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
- **Entity test:** Oklo (founded 2013), X-energy (2009), NuScale (2007), Kairos Power (2016), NANO Nuclear (founded 2022) and General Matter (founded 2024) are advanced-fission businesses; the separately financed, privately held General Matter qualifies as a startup. **Centrus: NOT A STARTUP**. It is the post-bankruptcy successor to USEC, a legacy uranium-enrichment enterprise with a material dedicated HALEU project. TerraPower (2006) develops independent reactor IP. Meta, Amazon, Google, TVA, AEP, Dow, DOE and the NRC are demand partners, utilities or institutions, **not** advanced-nuclear startup counts.
- **Source quotes:** short verbatim excerpts below support each raw input. Ratios, totals and changes labeled *derived* are our arithmetic and are disclosed in their candidate notes.
