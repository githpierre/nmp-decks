```yaml
status: ok
title: Why has it become commoditized?
note: We see commoditization where the industry can reuse proven fuel, machines and methods from today's reactor fleet. New value therefore comes from licences, scarce fuel, firm contracts and real construction.
t:
  7: LOW-ENRICHED FUEL
  9: STEAM TURBINES
  11: WATER COOLING
  13: CONCEPT DESIGNS
  15: SAFETY CODES
  17: COMPUTER MODELS
  19: NON-BINDING DEALS
  21: PUBLIC GRANTS
  6: Because a mature global industry has made this fuel for decades
  8: Because big makers like GE Vernova and Siemens build them for every plant
  10: Because decades of reactor builds made the design and parts widely known
  12: Because a concept costs little to draw next to a licensed reactor
  14: Because national regulators publish their codes for every applicant
  16: Because national labs and vendors sell or share modeling tools widely
  18: Because a memorandum costs nothing and binds no buyer to pay
  20: Because public programs spread small grants across many teams
```
