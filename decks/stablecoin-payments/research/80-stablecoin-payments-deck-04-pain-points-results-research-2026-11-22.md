### ed055
```yaml
status: ok
title: Which old problems are now behind us?
note: We treat these as problems stablecoins are designed to remove. They are not proof that every country or user can now pay with stablecoins at a low cost.
t:
  5: Businesses waited three to five days for an international wire to arrive
  7: Stablecoin transfers settle in minutes, including on weekends and holidays, and the receiver sees the money arrive
  8: Wires stopped at weekends, bank holidays and daily cut-off times
  10: Blockchains run all day, every day, so a payment never waits for a bank to open or for a cut-off time
  11: Firms needed a bank account in each country to hold local currencies
  13: One stablecoin account can hold digital dollars and pay out in many countries, with no bank account in each
  14: Stablecoin issuers had no clear rules on reserves or licenses
  16: The GENIUS Act and MiCA now set reserve, reporting and license rules for issuers
  17: Freelancers abroad could not get paid in dollars without a US bank
  19: Stablecoin accounts and payroll apps now pay them in digital dollars directly, to keep or cash out
  20: Crypto cards were rare and blocked by most card networks
  22: Visa and Mastercard now let partners issue cards that spend stablecoin balances
```
