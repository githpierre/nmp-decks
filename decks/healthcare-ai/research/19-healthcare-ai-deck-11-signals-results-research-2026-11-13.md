# Healthcare AI: evidence-backed signals for additional deck slides

Research cut-off: November 13, 2026. Ranked by the sum of surprise, importance, three-second clarity and six-month durability (maximum 20). All figures carry exact supporting source excerpts and chart-ready data. A `fresh` tag denotes the latest measured period, not the publication date.

**Scope and interpretation.** AI must be core to the healthcare or life-sciences offering. The physician-wide AMA survey is included only as a demand-side backdrop. Doximity is an incumbent comparator, not counted as an AI-first startup. Private valuations are financing marks, not real-time traded values. Potential biopharma milestone payments are not booked revenue. Startup funding rounds are excluded from deal selection because NewMarketPitch computes funding-round slides internally.

## Format screening: no defensible candidate retained

The following were screened for in-scope 2025-2026 evidence and not forced into the deck: **country-gap** (no comparable same-year cross-country adoption series), **waiting-line** (no verified queue/capacity pair), **incident-count** (no consistent annual harm reporting series), **announced-vs-built** (no comparable pledges and delivered counts), **idle-capacity** (no verified installed versus used capacity), **promise-tracker** (no three reliably dated slips of the same AI milestone), **lobbying-spend** (no striking LD-2 change tied uniquely to healthcare AI), **cost-threshold** (no consistent price time series crossing a clear benchmark), **adoption-race** (no independently comparable startup launch-to-user milestones), **earnings-mentions** (no controlled four-quarter FactSet/Iot Analytics series), **real-world-vs-funding** (the requested private funding series resides in NewMarketPitch's database), **incumbent-crash** and **fallen-star** (no validated split-adjusted quarter-close series), **challenger** (no reliable like-for-like private and public value series with shrinking gap), **dominance** (no multi-period same-provider market shares), **contract-share** (no comparable awards to entrants and incumbents), **revenue-per-head** (private employee counts not verified against the same revenue period), **dollar-split** (no published same-product unit economics), **single-customer** (no verified 30%+ named customer share), **forecast-gap** (no five comparable market-scope estimates for one year), and **funding-event** (funding rounds are handled separately).

The **funnel** uses counts of published studies and must not be presented as the actual commercial adoption funnel. The **rules-wave** is a regulated-device subset, not all AI healthcare products, and the 2026 bar is partial. The **profit-gap** is a GAAP net-loss-to-revenue calculation with revenue largely based on partnerships. The two OpenEvidence usage signals share a source and should not both be selected if the final slide deck needs topic diversity.
