  7: MagicSchool and Brisk now draft quizzes, rubrics and feedback with AI, and teachers review them first

  10: Speak and Duolingo now offer live AI voice and video calls that give feedback during the talk

  13: Instructure's Canvas Lite now lets any educator create a course and invite students for free
  14: Students had to write their own practice exams from their notes
  16: Quizlet now turns notes into full practice exams with chosen question types and difficulty
  17: Employees had to leave their work tools to find any course
  19: Coursera's learning agent now brings its courses inside Microsoft 365 Copilot at work
  20: Learners got little feedback after a live online lesson
  22: Preply's Lesson Insights now sum up each lesson, and three in four of its English learners use them
