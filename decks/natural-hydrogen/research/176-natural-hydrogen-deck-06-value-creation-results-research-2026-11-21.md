t:
  5: STIMULATED HYDROGEN
  10: EXCEPTIONAL CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
  7: The developer drills into iron-rich rock next to an ammonia plant, injects water and a catalyst, and gets a steady flow of pure hydrogen for under $1 per kilogram. More wells are added each year as the plant's demand grows.
  12: VS
  9: BAD CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
  6: The developer injects water into the rock, but the reaction is slow, the wells clog with minerals and the hydrogen comes out mixed with other gases. The pilot ends with costs far above hydrogen made in factories.
