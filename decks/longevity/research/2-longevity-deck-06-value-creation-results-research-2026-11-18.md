title: Why has it become commoditized?
note: We see commoditization where longevity companies reuse tests, devices and drugs that already exist at scale. New value therefore comes from proof in people, guidance and long-term member data.
t:
  7: STANDARD BLOOD TESTS
  9: WEARABLES
  11: NAD PILLS
  13: AGING CLOCKS
  15: GENOME TESTS
  17: WELLNESS APPS
  19: VIDEO VISITS
  21: GENERIC DRUGS
  6: Because big labs run millions of the same tests for hospitals every day
  8: Because phone makers and fitness brands sell them by the millions
  10: Because anyone can make and sell them online with few rules to follow
  12: Because research teams publish their methods for others to reuse
  14: Because the cost of reading DNA has fallen by thousands of times
  16: Because thousands of apps offer the same tracking features for free
  18: Because the pandemic made video visits a standard part of care
  20: Because their patents ran out long ago, so they cost very little
