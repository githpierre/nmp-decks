  8: Now it draws record funding and runs its own clinics in big cities
  10: Aging biology used to be studied only in university labs
  12: Now startups backed by billions run their own aging research
  14: Lab tests used to be ordered only by a doctor
  16: Now people buy 100 lab tests at once and see results in an app
  18: Anti-aging used to mean creams and cosmetics for the skin
  20: Now it means drugs, scans and tests that look inside the body
  22: Lifespan drugs used to stop at mouse studies with no buyer
  24: Now a drug for dog lifespan has passed two key FDA review steps
  26: Prevention used to be free advice from a family doctor
  28: Now people pay thousands for a full body checkup and a plan
