## Editorial decisions and remaining limitations

- The seven Market opportunity candidates all satisfy the specified freshness window. Historical ART, UK IVF funding, congenital syphilis and FY2025 revenue intensity are deliberately allocated to other chapters, not promoted as fresh momentum.
- The report should select at most a subset of the three employer-benefits signals (Progyny, SHRM, Mercer) and the Maven scale signal if editorial repetition is a concern. They measure different things: signed lives, menopause support, IVF coverage and network reach.
- The country-gap comparison uses constituent UK countries; it is conditional on this editorial interpretation of country. The waiting-line chart uses observed monthly throughput as a capacity proxy. The KFF behavioral rate and SHRM support rate are demand proxies, not direct product-adoption data.
- The deal timeline is a selected event cluster, not an exhaustive M&A census. Contracted lives, eligible populations, and announced transactions should not be portrayed as realized treatment volume or closed deal value.
- No internal funding data was supplied. No round-by-round funding data was researched or fabricated to populate the two funding-dependent formats.
- Exact publication dates could not be established for every report or live page; month-level dates and access dates are retained rather than invented. Quotes from numeric tables preserve the visible labels and values with normalized whitespace; they are excerpts, not complete prose sentences.
- Source verification and threshold review support this shortlist, but do not establish that no other candidate exists for the excluded formats.
