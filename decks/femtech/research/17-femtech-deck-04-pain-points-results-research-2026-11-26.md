### ed055
```yaml
status: ok
title: Which old problems are now behind us?
note: We treat these as problems that new products and benefits have removed for many women. They do not mean that care is now easy to reach or paid for everywhere.
t:
  5: Tracking a cycle meant a paper calendar and guesses about fertile days
  7: Apps such as Flo, Clue and Natural Cycles now track cycles and predict fertile days for tens of millions of women
  8: Almost every couple paid for fertility treatment out of their own pocket
  10: Many large employers now pay for IVF and egg freezing through benefits from Progyny, Carrot or Maven Clinic
  11: In the US, birth control pills always required a visit to a doctor first
  13: Opill became the first US birth control pill sold without a prescription, approved by the FDA in July 2023
  14: Breast pumps were loud and wired, so mothers had to sit still to pump
  16: Wearable pumps from Elvie and Willow fit inside a bra, so mothers can pump quietly while they move and work
  17: Women had few doctors trained to treat the symptoms of menopause
  19: Virtual clinics such as Midi Health now offer menopause care by video, with visits billed to health insurance
  20: Pelvic floor training meant repeated visits to a specialist clinic
  22: FDA-cleared home devices such as Elvie Trainer guide pelvic floor exercises and track each session's progress
```
