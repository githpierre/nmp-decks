```yaml
title: Which synthetic biology truths have flipped?
note: These are market shifts we see in recent deals, failures and company releases. They are not formal industry rules, but they come back across the evidence.
t:
  6: Synthetic biology used to promise to replace oil chemicals
  8: Now most money goes to medicines, food proteins and farming microbes
  10: Biofoundries used to sell only their design work to clients
  12: Now Ginkgo also sells its robots and data as separate products
  14: Protein design was once slow, random trial and error
  16: Now AI models propose working designs before the lab tests them
  18: Startups used to build their own big factories from day one
  20: Now most startups rent tanks from contract makers before building
  22: Engineered cell medicines used to stop at the lab bench
  24: Now Casgevy sells gene-edited cells and more programs near approval
  26: The field used to be led by fast-growing listed platforms
  28: Now Ginkgo trades far below its 2021 peak and value moves to startups
```
