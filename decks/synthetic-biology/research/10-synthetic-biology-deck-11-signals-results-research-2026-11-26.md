## Format screening and editorial checks

Retained 13 candidates across 10 formats, including seven Market opportunity slides. The candidate count is deliberately limited to supported examples. The two separate Ginkgo slides address different issues (historical shareholder losses versus current operating economics); choose one if the deck needs more company diversity. The two precision-fermentation slides are complementary but also share EVERY; choose the KPI slide if only one is needed.

| Format | Decision and threshold check |
|---|---|
| key-trend | Keep CASGEVY (5.82x) and Twist AI customer revenue (2.75x). |
| rules-wave | Drop. FDA and other agencies have fresh individual decisions, but a clean four-year, like-for-like series limited to engineered biology was not established. Broad cell and RNA approvals include non-engineered products. |
| funnel | Keep ASGCT/Citeline pipeline: 1,355 preclinical versus 11 pre-registration. This is a snapshot, not an attrition cohort. |
| country-gap | Keep CMN five-country clinical-trial comparison; 143 vs 33. More recent September preview gives only exact US and China counts publicly. |
| waiting-line | Drop. CASGEVY eligible population is not a waitlist; no comparable queue and throughput pair established. |
| incident-count | Drop. No sufficiently consistent official engineered-biology incident series established. |
| announced-vs-built | Keep Liberation: 600,000 liters planned, no operational capacity. |
| idle-capacity | Drop. Announced fermentation tank volume is not utilized volume; no reliable same-site utilization denominator. |
| promise-tracker | Drop. Liberation had several revised targets, but three independently verified slips for the identical milestone were not established. |
| lobbying-spend | Drop. No validated LD-2 series with a striking change established. |
| cost-threshold | Drop. Pivot Bio has reported a favorable price relative to conventional nitrogen, but four comparable dated prices and an identical reference were not established. |
| penetration-curve | Drop. Farm acreage or company counts are not comparable user penetration surveys. |
| adoption-race | Drop. No clean, like-for-like commercial milestone across companies demonstrating acceleration was established. |
| earnings-mentions | Drop. No FactSet or IoT Analytics quarterly series for synthetic biology verified. |
| real-world-vs-funding | Drop. Proprietary quarterly funding series not supplied; divergence cannot be tested. |
| incumbent-crash | Drop. No clear external market-event anchor and complete quarterly close series for a displaced incumbent. Ginkgo is better treated as a fallen star. |
