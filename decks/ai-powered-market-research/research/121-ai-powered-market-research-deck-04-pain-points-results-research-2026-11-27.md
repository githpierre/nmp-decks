### ed055
```yaml
status: ok
title: Which old problems are now behind us?
note: We treat these as problems that new tools have removed for many research teams. They do not mean that every study is now fast, cheap or reliable everywhere.
t:
  5: Each in-depth interview needed a human moderator and a booked time slot
  7: AI moderators from Outset, Strella and Conveo now run hundreds of interviews at once, day and night
  8: A qualitative study took six to eight weeks from brief to final report
  10: Listen Labs and Conveo report studies where the first findings arrive in a few days instead of weeks
  11: Open answers and transcripts were read and coded line by line by hand
  13: AI coding tools such as NVivo AI Cloud now group answers into themes and link each theme to its source
  14: A study in many countries needed a local agency and translators in each
  16: AI interviewers now run the same study in dozens of languages, and Conveo supports more than 50 of them
  17: Screening an idea meant paying for a full survey with real respondents
  19: Synthetic panels from Zappi, Evidenza and others now pre-screen many ideas before a real survey runs
  20: Past findings sat in old slide decks that no one could find again
  22: Listen Labs and Conveo build searchable archives, so each new interview adds to past customer evidence
```
