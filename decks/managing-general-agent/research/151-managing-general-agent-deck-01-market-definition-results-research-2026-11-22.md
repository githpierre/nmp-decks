title: What is the MGA market, in simple terms?
text: MGAs are specialist firms that price and sell insurance on behalf of big insurers, under their license, while those insurers and their reinsurers carry most of the risk
note: We use a conversational definition here because it makes the boundary with brokers, who only place cover, and with insurers, who keep the risk, easier to understand.

t:

  11: A carrier keeps the risk on its own balance sheet and needs a license and capital, which most MGAs do not hold
