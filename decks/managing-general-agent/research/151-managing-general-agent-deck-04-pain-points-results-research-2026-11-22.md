### ed055
```yaml
status: ok
title: Which old problems are now behind us?
note: We treat these as problems that software, data and new capacity sources have removed for the best MGAs. They do not mean that every MGA now prices risk well.
t:
  5: Brokers waited days for a price on any risk outside the standard box
  7: Tech-led MGAs such as Ledgebrook now send firm quotes to wholesale brokers in minutes from one shared system
  8: Insurers saw delegated books once a quarter, in messy spreadsheets
  10: Lloyd's and insurers now ask for standard data feeds, so they can watch an MGA's book almost live
  11: Cyber insurance was sold blind, with no look at a firm's defenses
  13: Coalition and At-Bay now scan each client's systems before quoting and warn them of new threats
  14: Home insurers left wildfire zones with no way to price each house
  16: Stand now models each home and gives discounts of up to 70% to homes made more resilient
  17: Truck insurance was priced on past claims and a few forms only
  19: Nirvana and Cover Whale now price fleets from live vehicle data and safety cameras
  20: A new MGA could only find capacity at Lloyd's or a big insurer
  22: Fronting carriers and platforms such as Accelerant now lend their paper to hundreds of MGAs
```
