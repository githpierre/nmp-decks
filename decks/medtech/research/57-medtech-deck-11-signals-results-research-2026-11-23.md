**Reading the file:** Each slide candidate is self-contained YAML with all chart points, source URLs, short source excerpts, source date and freshness metadata. **Scope checks:** FDA clearances are not patient adoption; FDA Breakthrough designation is not marketing authorization; repeated entries in the FDA AI list are not unique solutions; public and older companies in the deal timeline are retained as NOT A STARTUP, not mislabeled as venture startups. Clinical diagnostic viewing software is in scope only where its direct medical use is FDA-cleared.

## Production cautions

1. **The FDA funnel is not a conversion rate.** The designation and authorization totals are differently aged populations, and lagging applications are right-censored. The visual should read "cumulative program tallies" rather than "84% failed".
2. **The FDA AI inventory is a curated list of decision records.** Do not equate 1,614 listings with 1,614 independently deployed devices. The same product can be authorized more than once and the latest complete decision date is June 29, 2026.
3. **Do not combine dollar values in the deal timeline.** Some are enterprise value; others cash consideration, closing price or announced base payment. Transactions are not necessarily closed; the Penumbra, Xodus, Proscia and Contego announcements must retain their announcement status unless a later filing confirms close. 5. **Neuralink has an imprecise historical baseline.** The November 2026 report gives seven patients in the previous year but no exact month. The 26-patient figure is an attributed interview statement, not a regulatory or trial-registry cohort report. Keep lower priority than primary-source company and FDA series.
6. **The operating-margin chart is a scale contrast, not a like-for-like product comparison.** Each company addresses a different clinical device category and has a different growth phase.
