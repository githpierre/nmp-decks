### ed055
```yaml
status: ok
title: Which old problems are now behind us?
note: We treat these as problems that AI models, robotic labs and new rules have removed from early discovery. They do not mean that drugs now succeed more often in late trials.
t:
  5: Chemists had to make and test thousands of molecules to find one good lead
  7: AI now ranks billions of virtual molecules, so labs only make the few hundred that look most promising
  8: Finding the 3D shape of a protein could take a lab years of work
  10: AlphaFold now predicts the shape of almost any protein in minutes, and its free database holds over 200 million
  11: New antibodies came from immunizing animals and screening what came out
  13: Chai, Generate and Xaira now design antibodies on a computer against a chosen spot of the target
  14: Only big pharma could afford the computing and chemists for design
  16: Cloud models now let a biotech of ten people use design tools as good as those of a big pharma
  17: Toxic molecules were often found late, in costly animal tests
  19: AI toxicity models and the FDA plan of 2025 on animal tests move safety checks earlier
  20: Finding new uses for old drugs relied on lucky observations
  22: AI now scans approved and dropped drugs against new diseases, as Biossil does with abandoned candidates
```
